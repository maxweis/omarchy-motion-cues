[Overview]

## Choose an app
GyrOSC: recommended for iPhone because it offers background mode.
In GyrOSC settings, enable “run in background” and select 30 Hz (30 updates per second).
phyphox: free on iPhone or Android; keep the app open and phone unlocked.

## Get connected
Use the same private Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.
Follow your app's tab, then enable Motion Cues.
Auto-detect selects the available feed after setup.

## Phone position
Flat: screen up, top edge forward.
Upright: top edge up, screen facing you as you face forward.
Secure the phone; match Phone position in the menu.

Passenger use only. Stop if uncomfortable.

[phyphox setup]

## On your phone
1. Open Acceleration (without g). Tap Play.
2. Turn on Allow remote access; allow Local Network permission.
3. Copy the shown http:// address, including its port.

Keep phyphox open and the phone unlocked.

## On your laptop
4. Motion Cues > Phone address…: enter the phone's address.
5. Choose Enable.

No bubbles? Check Connection status, Play, and the phone address.

[GyrOSC setup]

## Required: firewall
Allow incoming UDP from your phone to the laptop IP/port above. Not added automatically. Keep the firewall enabled.

UFW example; replace every placeholder:
sudo ufw allow in on INTERFACE proto udp from PHONE_IP to LAPTOP_IP port PORT

Update the rule if addresses change; remove it when no longer needed.

## In GyrOSC
1. Enter the laptop's Wi-Fi/hotspot IP and port above, not the phone IP.
2. Enable unscaled acceleration (/gyrosc/accel), default “gyrosc” tag.
3. Allow Motion & Fitness and Local Network permissions.
4. On the laptop, choose Motion Cues > Enable.

## Recommended iPhone settings
In GyrOSC settings, enable “run in background” and select 30 Hz (30 updates per second).
Test other apps and locking; reliability varies. One update per second is too slow for this plugin.
No bubbles? Check Connection status, IP/port, 30 Hz, and firewall.
